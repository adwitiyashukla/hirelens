Senior Backend Engineer, Payments Platform

We are hiring a Senior Backend Engineer to own the services behind our payments
platform. You will design, ship and operate systems that process millions of
events per day, and you will be on call for what you build.

What you will do
- Own backend services end to end, from design through production operation
- Improve the performance and reliability of high-traffic payment paths
- Work with event streaming infrastructure at scale

Requirements
- Strong experience running containerised workloads in production
- Experience with high-throughput event streaming systems such as Kafka
- A track record of measurably improving system performance
- Comfortable owning services in production, including on-call

Nice to have
- Experience building distributed systems from first principles
- Open source contributions
- Infrastructure as code

We offer a competitive salary, generous equity, free lunch, and a hybrid working
policy. We are an equal opportunity employer.
